How it works

From a visit recording to a draft SOAP note.

This is the Cosmas workflow available today.

1. Upload the recording

After the visit, upload its recording from your signed-in Cosmas practice.

2. Review the draft

Cosmas creates a draft SOAP note. The clinician reviews and edits it before use.

3. Copy into the chart

Copy and paste the reviewed note into the patient chart. Cosmas does not write to the EHR.
