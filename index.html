How it works today

From the visit to a note you would sign.

This is the Cosmas workflow available today.

1. Record the visit and upload it

Record the visit with your own recorder, with the patient’s consent, then upload it from your signed-in Cosmas practice.

2. Review the draft

Cosmas writes a draft SOAP note. You review and edit it before it is used.

3. Paste it into your chart

Copy and paste the reviewed note into the patient chart. Cosmas does not write to your EHR. Your chart stays yours.

Where the platform is going

One agent at a time, each with a clinician in the loop.

Cosmas is being built as a platform of agents for the work around the visit. The draft note is the first one available. The rest are in development and are not offered today; each is released only when a clinician can review every draft it produces.
