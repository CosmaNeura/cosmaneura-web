Business Associate Agreement

Covered entity: the practice you name in Cosmas. Business associate: Cosmaneura Inc. Product: Cosmas. This clickwrap text is a product-UX draft. Counsel must ratify before it is the operative legal instrument.

1. Parties

This BAA is between Cosmaneura Inc, a Delaware corporation (“Business Associate”), and the practice named at acceptance (“Covered Entity”). Cosmas is the service operated by Cosmaneura Inc.

2. One agreement for the practice

This BAA is with the practice, not with each clinician. Workforce may use Cosmas under this BAA. Invited members attest; they do not execute a second BAA.

3. Permitted uses

Cosmaneura Inc may use and disclose PHI only to provide Cosmas (ambient capture, draft documentation, coding suggestions, intake, and related workflows), for proper management, legal duties, and data aggregation, each as permitted by 45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e). Cosmaneura Inc will not use PHI for marketing or sell PHI.

4. Fail-closed

Cosmaneura Inc will not process PHI through Cosmas until this BAA is accepted. Cosmas does not write autonomously to an EHR; a human remains the transmitting actor.

5. Term

Effective on acceptance in Cosmas. Questions: info@cosmaneura.com.

Joining a practice also requires a matching work-email domain (for MyCatholicDoctor: @mycatholicdoctor.com).
