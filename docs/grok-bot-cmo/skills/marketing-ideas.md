# Skill: marketing-ideas (Cosmas)

Use when brainstorming campaigns or experiments.

1. Filter ideas through PHI, claim-scope, and static-site constraints.
2. Discard ideas that need claim submission, EHR write-back, or PHI scraping.
3. Prefer practice-owner education, door-specific outreach, and CRO tests.
4. Output: 10 ideas max, each with channel, effort, and risk note.
