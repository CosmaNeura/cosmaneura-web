Before you get started

A clear first step.

Where do I create a CosmaBot account?

CosmaBot signup starts on cosmas.ai. That is a different product door from Cosmas.

Is Cosmas the same as CosmaBot?

No. Cosmas is the recording-to-draft SOAP workflow. CosmaBot is the AI coworker team. Creating a Cosmas practice account does not unlock CosmaBot, and CosmaBot signup does not create a Cosmas account.

How do I continue with Cosmas instead?

Use Cosmas practice signup or explore the current Cosmas workflow. Cosmas list pricing stays on the homepage.
