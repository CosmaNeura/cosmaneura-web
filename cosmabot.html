Meet CosmaBot.

Cosmaneura Inc is developing an AI coworker team for healthcare practices. CosmaBot brings a planned Provider Team around the work before, during, and after a visit, while your practice remains responsible for review and decisions.

Explore CosmaBot & the waitlistContact John

One company. A growing set of practice tools.

Cosmas is the existing recording-to-draft-SOAP workflow from Cosmaneura Inc. A clinician reviews and edits the draft, then copies it into the chart. CosmaBot is a planned team experience that builds around a broader practice day. Its public product home is cosmas.ai.

The CosmaBot waitlist is for contact information and product interest. Joining does not create a clinical account or provide access to the planned team experience.

The planned Provider Team

Visit Prep

Organize selected context and questions before the visit, ready for your team’s review.

Scribe

Help organize a conversation into a draft note for clinician review and editing.

Follow-up

Draft next steps and messages for the practice to approve before sharing.

Chief

Help organize team work and surface the items that need a human decision.

Research

Explore reviewed research questions with sources, dates, and evidence limits. Private context is not automatically forwarded to public research services.

Billing Support

Organize documentation questions and billing checklists for review. Advisory support only, with no claim submission or reimbursement promises.

These roles describe the planned CosmaBot experience. Release timing, early-access eligibility, and final capabilities will be announced on cosmas.ai.
