Before you get started

A clear first step.

What do I get when I create an account?

Account setup begins with the current Cosmas recording-to-draft-SOAP workflow. You authenticate and complete practice onboarding. No card is collected during onboarding. See current list pricing.

Does an account give me CosmaBot access?

The CosmaBot team experience is coming soon. Creating a practice account does not unlock the planned team, reserve a price, or guarantee early access.

How do I hear about the CosmaBot launch?

Visit cosmas.ai for product details and waitlist availability. Launch emails are a separate, optional signup with email confirmation. Creating an account here does not subscribe you to launch emails.
